## 2. Apex Government Bodies & National Regulatory Fellowships

Prestigious public sector opportunities with official Government of India certifications, high policy impact, and strong resume value for higher studies (MS/MBA/UPSC).

### 2.1 NITI Aayog Internship Scheme
- **Role:** Policy, AI, and Data Analytics Intern
- **Location:** New Delhi, India (Hybrid)
- **Stipend:** Certificate of Excellence & Direct Government Experience
- **Application Window:** Open from the **1st to 10th of every month** on the official portal.
- **Eligibility:** Undergraduate/postgraduate students with minimum 85% in 12th / aggregate university standing.
- **Application Portal:** [NITI Aayog Portal](https://www.niti.gov.in/internship)
- **Hub Slug:** `niti-aayog-internship`
- **Tags:** `Government`, `Policy`, `Data Science`, `AI Governance`, `New Delhi`

### 2.2 SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India)

- **Stipend:** ₹30,000 – ₹45,000 / month
- **Eligibility:** Engineering students specializing in Computer Science, IT, Cybersecurity, Data Analytics, or Financial Engineering.
- **Application Portal:** [SEBI Careers](https://www.sebi.gov.in/)
- **Hub Slug:** `sebi-it-internship`
- **Tags:** `SEBI`, `Government`, `Cybersecurity`, `Financial Markets`, `Mumbai`

## 3. Global Remote Open-Source Fellowships (Paid in USD)

Fully remote, merit-based programs open to Indian students without requiring on-campus recruitment.

### 3.1 LFX Mentorship (Linux Foundation)
- **Role:** Open Source Software Engineering Mentee (Linux Kernel, CNCF, Kubernetes, Hyperledger)
- **Location:** 100% Remote
- **Stipend:** $3,000 – $6,600 USD (~₹2,50,000 – ₹5,50,000 total)
- **Cohorts:** Spring (March–May), Summer (June–August), Fall (Sept–Nov).
- **Eligibility:** Proficiency in Go, Rust, C, Python, or distributed systems. Active GitHub profile.
- **Application Portal:** [LFX Mentorship Portal](https://mentorship.lfx.linuxfoundation.org/)
- **Hub Slug:** `lfx-mentorship`
- **Tags:** `Open Source`, `Remote`, `Linux Foundation`, `Kubernetes`, `USD Stipend`

### 3.2 Outreachy
- **Role:** Open Source Contributor & Software Intern
- **Location:** 100% Remote
- **Stipend:** $7,000 USD (~₹5,80,000 total) + $1,000 travel/equipment allowance
- **Cohorts:** May to August (Summer) & December to March (Winter)
- **Eligibility:** Anyone who faces under-representation or systemic bias in the tech industry.
- **Application Portal:** [Outreachy Website](https://www.outreachy.org/)
- **Hub Slug:** `outreachy-remote-internship`
- **Tags:** `Open Source`, `Remote`, `USD Stipend`, `Diversity in Tech`, `Full Stack`

### 3.3 Major League Hacking (MLH) Fellowship
- **Role:** MLH Fellow (Software Engineering / Open Source / Web3 Tracks)
- **Location:** 100% Remote
- **Stipend:** Monthly Educational Grant (Needs-adjusted)
- **Cohorts:** Spring, Summer, and Fall (12 weeks full-time or part-time)
- **Eligibility:** Demonstrated coding ability, open to all undergraduate students.
- **Application Portal:** [MLH Fellowship](https://fellowship.mlh.io/)
- **Hub Slug:** `mlh-fellowship`
- **Tags:** `Open Source`, `Remote`, `MLH`, `Web3`, `Software Engineering`

### 3.4 Summer of Bitcoin
- **Role:** Bitcoin Protocol & Cryptographic Engineering Intern
- **Location:** 100% Remote
- **Stipend:** $2,50,0 – $4,000 USD
- **Timeline:** May – August annually
- **Eligibility:** Open to university students globally. C++, Rust, Python, Bitcoin Core, and Lightning Network.
- **Application Portal:** [Summer of Bitcoin](https://www.summerofbitcoin.org/)
- **Hub Slug:** `summer-of-bitcoin-internship`
- **Tags:** `Bitcoin`, `Crypto`, `Rust`, `Remote`, `USD Stipend`
